The job

The count comes from the brick PLUS its 3/8 in joint - a "modular" brick is 7-5/8 in long but lays at 8. Pavers are sand-set, so no mortar; walls get a bag per 35 bricks.

Your order

Materials only. Walls also need ties, flashing, and weep vents; patios need base gravel, bedding sand, and edge restraint. The brick is the visible half of the bill.

What the pallet will not tell you

"About 7 per square foot" is the joint talkinga modular brick lays at 8 x 2-5/8 with its joint - count from the laid size, never the brick alone, or you come up 5% short
Waste is bond-dependent, not luckherringbone cuts every edge brick twice; running bond barely cuts at all - 15% vs 10% is the honest spread
Mortar dies on the boarda 70 lb bag lays about 35 bricks and lives about 2 hours; mixing a day's worth at once is how walls get weak joints
Bricks vary by the cube, not the piececolor shifts between production runs - order the whole job from one cube and open several at once to blend
Soldier courses eat bricks standing upa brick on edge covers a third less wall per piece - that is why the fancy top course costs what it costs
The pallet deposit is real moneyreturn the pallets and the banding; most yards refund $10-25 a pallet if they come back clean
